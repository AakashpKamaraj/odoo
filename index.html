Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about DayFlow and getting started.

Is my task data stored privately? ▼
Yes, completely! DayFlow stores all your tasks, streak data, and preferences locally in your browser with zero latency. You can also export or import your entire database at any time from your Profile settings.
How does the Pomodoro focus timer work with my tasks? ▼
DayFlow integrates a 25-minute Pomodoro timer directly into your daily agenda. You can choose any urgent task to lock in on, start the interval, and enjoy subtle audio cues upon session completion.
Can I use DayFlow offline on my mobile device or laptop? ▼
Absolutely. Because DayFlow is built with modern, lightweight web technologies and client-side persistence, it functions without requiring an active internet connection.
What are the keyboard shortcuts available? ▼
You can press ? at any time in the app to view all shortcuts. Use N for quick task creation, Space to toggle the Pomodoro timer, and T to switch light/dark theme.
